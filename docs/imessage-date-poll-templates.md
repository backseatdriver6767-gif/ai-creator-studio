# iMessage Date-Finder Templates (group of 10+)

## Option A — Native iMessage Poll (iOS 26+, everyone on blue bubbles)

1. Open the group chat → tap **+** next to the text box → **Polls**.
2. Question: `Which of these dates do NOT work for you? (tap every one you can't do)`
3. Add one choice per candidate date (max 12), e.g. `Sat Nov 22`, `Sun Nov 23`, `Sat Dec 6`...
4. Add a final choice: `All of these work for me ✅`
5. Send. Pin the poll (long-press → Pin) so it stays at the top.

Reading results: the date with **zero votes** wins. Tap the poll to see who voted for what,
so you can nudge anyone who hasn't voted yet.

## Option B — Tapback method (works on any iPhone, no iOS 26 needed)

Message 1 (send this first):
> Picking a date for ___. I'm sending each date as its own message below.
> 👎 Tapback every date you CAN'T do. No reaction = you're free.
> Please get to it by ___. Fewest 👎 wins.

Then send each date as a separate message:
> Sat Nov 22
> Sun Nov 23
> Sat Dec 6
> Sun Dec 7

Reading results: tap the reaction count under each date to see exactly who can't make it.

## Option C — Copy-paste checklist (fallback, works even with green bubbles)

> Reply to this with the dates you CAN'T do (just copy, delete the ones that work):
> ❌ Sat Nov 22
> ❌ Sun Nov 23
> ❌ Sat Dec 6
> ❌ Sun Dec 7
> ✅ All work for me

## Tips
- Offer 4–8 dates. More than that and people stop reading.
- Ask for "can't do" rather than "can do": people answer faster and non-responders default to available.
- Set a deadline in the message and @mention stragglers.
- Add "or 'any weekend works'" to catch flexible people.
