# Placement Diagnostic

**40 questions · 8 sections · no calculator · no notes · no time limit**

---

## Before you start — read this

This exam has exactly one purpose: to find out where to start. It is not graded,
not recorded, and not a judgment of you. A score that flatters you costs you
months, because it places you above a gap that then silently breaks everything
downstream.

Rules:

1. **No calculator, no notes, no searching.** If you can't do it from memory, you
   can't do it — that's information we need.
2. **No studying beforehand.** Take it cold, today.
3. **Attempt everything.** A wrong attempt tells us more than a blank, because it
   shows where your reasoning goes.
4. **Write "GUESS"** next to any answer you weren't confident in, even if you got
   it right. A lucky guess in Section 5 is a Section 5 gap.
5. **Handwrite your work** on separate paper and keep it. We're going to read the
   work, not just the answers — *how* you get a wrong answer is the diagnostic
   signal.

Expect this to take 60–120 minutes. Take it in one sitting if you can.

## Section 1 — Arithmetic & Fraction Fluency

**1.** Compute: `-3 - (-7) + (-2)`

**2.** Compute: `2/3 - 5/6 + 1/4`

**3.** Simplify: `(3/4) ÷ (9/8)`

**4.** Evaluate: `-2^4 + (-2)^4`

**5.** Compute: `5 - 2[3 - (4 - 7)]`

## Section 2 — Exponents & Radicals

**6.** Simplify to a single expression with positive exponents: `(x^3 · y^-2)^2 / (x^-1 · y^4)`

**7.** Simplify: `√50 + √18`

**8.** Evaluate: `8^(2/3)`

**9.** Rationalize the denominator: `1 / (2 - √3)`

**10.** Simplify: `(27x^6)^(1/3)`

## Section 3 — Linear Equations, Inequalities & Systems

**11.** Solve for x: `3(x - 2) - 4 = 2(x + 5)`

**12.** Solve for y: `2x - 3y = 12`

**13.** Solve and express in interval notation: `-2 < 3 - x ≤ 5`

**14.** Solve the system: `2x + 3y = 7` and `x - y = -4`

**15.** Solve: `|2x - 1| = 7`

## Section 4 — Factoring & Quadratics

**16.** Factor completely: `3x^3 - 12x`

**17.** Factor: `6x^2 - x - 12`

**18.** Solve exactly (no decimals): `x^2 - 6x + 4 = 0`

**19.** Factor: `x^3 - 8`

**20.** Write in vertex form by completing the square: `y = x^2 + 10x + 7`

## Section 5 — Rational Expressions

**21.** Simplify: `(x^2 - 9) / (x^2 - x - 6)`

**22.** Combine into a single fraction: `1/x + 1/(x + 1)`

**23.** Simplify the complex fraction: `(1/x - 1/3) / (x - 3)`

**24.** Solve for x: `2/(x - 1) = 3/(x + 2)`

**25.** Divide: `(x^3 - 2x^2 + 3x - 1) ÷ (x - 1)`

## Section 6 — Functions

**26.** Given `f(x) = x^2 - 3x`, find and simplify `f(a + 1)`.

**27.** State the domain of `f(x) = √(x - 2) / (x - 5)`.

**28.** Given `f(x) = 2x + 1` and `g(x) = x^2`, find and simplify `f(g(x)) - g(f(x))`.

**29.** Find the inverse of `f(x) = (2x + 1)/(x - 3)`.

**30.** Describe, in order, every transformation taking the graph of `y = f(x)` to `y = -2f(x - 3) + 1`.

## Section 7 — Exponentials & Logarithms

**31.** Solve: `2^(x + 1) = 32`

**32.** Evaluate: `log₂(1/8)`

**33.** Condense to a single logarithm: `2ln(x) - ln(y) + (1/2)ln(z)`

**34.** Solve: `log(x) + log(x - 3) = 1`

**35.** Solve for t exactly: `500 = 100e^(0.05t)`

## Section 8 — Geometry & Trigonometry

**37.** Evaluate exactly: `cos(2π/3)`

**38.** Evaluate exactly: `sin(7π/6)`

**39.** Simplify: `(1 - cos²θ) / sinθ`

**40.** Solve on the interval `[0, 2π)`: `2sin(x) - √3 = 0`
